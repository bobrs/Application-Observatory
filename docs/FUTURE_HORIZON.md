# Future horizon — possibilities, not MVP obligations

These directions are intentionally out of scope for v0.1. Their presence here does not authorize implementation.

- Broader application portfolios, multiple teams and independent reviewer identities.
- Proposal/idea lifecycle before code: request, DevSecOps boundaries, sponsor approval for bounded exploration, versioned refinements.
- Live source attribution from GitHub, build pipelines, deployment systems, API responses, manual reports, carefully bounded scrapes and AI-derived interpretations.
- Versioned and attributable Test results, QA, release criteria, promotion execution and rollback.
- Production health, incident history, database backup history and restore evidence, lifecycle costs, scale-back, and retirement obligations.
- Approved machine-readable organizational technology paths and contextual questions at feature/dependency registration.
- AI-assisted semantic explanation and deliberation, preserving uncertainty, dissent and authority boundaries.
- Cloudflare, Podman, Kubernetes, Ansible, and other environment-specific connectors when actual use warrants them.
- Multi-application executive views and continued stewardship across an application's entire lifetime.

## Permanent conceptual guardrails

Do not presume that a policy file exhausts human intent. Keep **observation, interpretation, decision, and consequence** distinct. Prefer bounded autonomy and evidence proportional to real consequences, not friction proportional to novelty.

The portal may witness its own evolution but should never be the sole authority asserting its own correctness.
